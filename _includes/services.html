Our Programs

Roots2Harvest Foundation is our community and research engine — holding educational initiatives, building pathways for community entrepreneurship, and leading community-based participatory research.

Community Education Series

A monthly series demonstrating growing practices and the challenges of urban agriculture in the South. Each session is led by experienced growers — master gardeners and farmers from the region — providing hands-on learning rooted in local knowledge and conditions.

NRG — Inner-G Program

"What you put your energy into GROWS." NRG employs and trains individuals stigmatized by previous convictions in urban agriculture. Each cohort partners together to establish and operate an agricultural or value-added business — with every member as a co-owner. Workforce development rooted in dignity and shared wealth.

Community-Based Participatory Research

We lead research measuring how fresh food intervention impacts our communities — at the level of environmental resilience, community connectedness and social health, and individual mental and physiological health. Our current focus is the restoration of urban soils for agricultural safety in Birmingham.
